EBP Store Labels connects your iPhone to your EBP software to print price labels in store, quickly and without typing errors.

LIVE EBP CATALOG
• Access all your EBP items from your phone
• Search by name or reference
• Filters: family, sub-family, price, availability, active items
• Sort by reference, name or price

COMPLETE ITEM SHEET
• Retail, net price and VAT
• Actual and available stock
• Aisle, family, supplier, logistics
• EAN-13 barcode

BLUETOOTH PRINTING
• Pocket 5890, Brother and NETUM thermal printers
• Label preview before printing
• Choose format and quantity
• No Internet connection needed to print

FAST SCAN
• Scan a barcode to instantly find the item

DEMO MODE
• Try the app with sample data, no account or connection required
