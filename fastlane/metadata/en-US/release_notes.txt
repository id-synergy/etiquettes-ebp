This update improves item search and the product sheet.

Search:
- Search now covers barcode and reference, not just the item name.
- A full barcode goes straight to the matching item instead of a long list.
- New "Sale unit" filter and new sort by stock.
- The Sort section is now at the top of the filters sheet, visible right away.
- Smoother typing: the keyboard stays up and the search waits until you finish typing.

Product sheet:
- Long-press any field (name, reference, barcode, price, stock…) to copy it in one gesture.
